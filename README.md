🌊 Water Simulation Sandbox

An interactive 2D water simulation built for the browser that lets you experiment with fluid-like behavior in real time. The simulation is based on cellular/particle dynamics and allows users to manipulate the environment while observing how water flows and reacts.

🔹 Features

💧 Spawn Water: Add new water particles anywhere in the grid

🧱 Place Walls: Draw barriers to redirect or trap water

🧪 Mix & Experiment: Interact with particles for sandbox-style play

⚙️ Custom Variables: Adjust physics parameters (gravity, flow speed, viscosity, spread, etc.) to change the way water behaves

🎨 Interactive UI: Simple controls for building, spawning, and tweaking the simulation

Perfect for learning fluid dynamics concepts, sandbox experiments, or just watching satisfying water patterns form and flow.
